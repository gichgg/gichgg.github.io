The Problem

Drowning in Data.
Starving for Insight.

Most HR teams sit on mountains of people data — and still make decisions by gut feel, because the data never tells them what to do next.

Backward-looking reports

Your quarterly HR report tells you what happened three months ago. By the time you see the attrition spike, your best engineers are already interviewing elsewhere.

Spreadsheet fragmentation

Engagement data lives in one place. Recruitment metrics in another. Performance reviews in a third. Nobody has a complete picture — and connecting the dots takes weeks, not minutes.
