The problem I solve

Most HR teams don't need more data. They need to stop producing it by hand.

If a capable person is copying numbers between systems to produce something, that something can almost certainly be generated instead.

Reporting

Reporting that rebuilds itself

The monthly pack, the assessment write-up, the board slide. Generated from the source data on a schedule, formatted the way you already present it, with no one retyping anything.

Dashboards

Dashboards people actually open

Workforce trends, talent pipeline, 9-Box succession analytics. Pulled from where the data already lives, refreshed without anyone touching it, and built around the decision rather than the metric.

Workflow

Workflows with the friction removed

Screening, ATS funnel optimisation, scheduling, structured notes, candidate summaries. The repetitive middle of a hiring process automated to the point where your team only handles the judgement calls.
