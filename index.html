Gicheha Gitau

Talent advisor by day, AI tinkerer by night. I'm Gich Gitau (GG). A consultant in Talent & Analytics based in Nairobi, specialising in people analytics, executive recruitment, and talent advisory.

I build people analytics infrastructure, KPI dashboards, and automation tools that surface better talent decisions — and experiment with AI to turn repetitive HR processes into smart, scalable apps.

My work spans psychometric assessment interpretation, executive search analytics, and building evidence-based hiring frameworks for Africa's leading organisations.

This site is a living portfolio — dashboards and analytics projects across recruitment, retention, engagement, and talent development. Advanced user and trainer of trainers for Hogan, Korn Ferry, Symbiotics, Talogy, Clevry, PQ, and custom assessment designer.
